# Gourmise Data Sources and Methods

Gourmise uses published government and university extension guidance when a reliable source exists. Other tools use standard mathematical conversions or clearly labeled planning estimates. Cooking results are never guarantees. Appliances, ingredients, food shape, starting temperature, altitude, and equipment all affect the result. Users must verify safe doneness with a food thermometer.

## Government food-safety and storage sources

- [FoodSafety.gov safe minimum internal temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures)
- [FoodSafety.gov meat and poultry roasting charts](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/meat-poultry-charts)
- [FoodSafety.gov cold food storage chart](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts)
- [FDA refrigerator and freezer storage chart](https://www.fda.gov/media/74435/download)
- [USDA FSIS freezing and food safety](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/freezing-and-food-safety)

## University extension sources

- [Colorado State University Extension high-elevation food preparation guide](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/high-elevation-food-preparation-guide/)
- [Purdue Extension slow cooker conversion guidance](https://extension.purdue.edu/foodlink/includes/pubs/Tips%20for%20Crock%20Pot%20Success.pdf)

## Measurement and ingredient sources

- [NIST Handbook 44 current edition](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/nist-handbook-44-current-edition)
- [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/)

Temperature conversion, unit conversion, recipe scaling, baker's percentages, bill splitting, and scheduling use standard formulas. Air fryer conversions, carryover rise, oven-change buffers, holding quality, equipment capacity, ingredient density, and yield equivalents are practical estimates, not government safety limits.
