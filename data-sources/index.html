Gourmise Data Sources

Gourmise uses published government and university extension guidance when a reliable source exists. Other tools use standard math or clearly labeled planning estimates. Cooking results are never guarantees. Always verify safe doneness with a food thermometer.

What is estimated

Air fryer conversions, carryover rise, oven-change buffers, holding quality, equipment capacity, ingredient density, and yield equivalents are practical starting points. They are not government safety limits or guaranteed appliance results.
