Camera access

Camera access is requested only when you start a scan or turn on Hands-Free Wave during Cook Along. If access was denied, open Settings, choose Apps, choose Gourmise, and enable Camera. You can also choose existing photos for cookbook, package, and grocery-list scans.

iCloud Sync

Turn on iCloud Sync in Gourmise Settings, then close and reopen the app. Use the same Apple Account on each device. Check the status in Settings; enabling the switch does not prove your whole library has uploaded. Sync timing is managed by iCloud and may not be instant. Keep a separate library export.

Household sharing

Open Settings, then Household Sharing. This is separate from syncing your own devices. Invite only people you want to share recipes, plans, or groceries with. Participants need iCloud access on a supported Apple device. Use Apple's share controls to manage access.

Units

Settings lets you choose Fahrenheit or Celsius and US or metric measurements separately. System follows your device region. Original recipe directions and amounts stay as written.

Saved data needs recovery

If Gourmise cannot open your library, editing and importing are paused to protect the saved files. Do not delete the app or reset its data. Install any available update and try opening it again. Contact support if the warning continues.

Back up your library

Open Gourmise Settings, choose Backup & Export, then select Export Full Library Backup. The backup includes your recipes, photos, original scans, ratings, notes, meal plans, grocery lists, favorites, and cooking history.

OCR results need correction

Use a bright, sharp photo with the page or instruction panel filling the frame. Avoid glare and heavy shadows. Gourmise always shows recognized recipe and grocery content for review before saving. Package scanning asks you to choose a cooking method when more than one method is found.

Food safety

Cooking times, appliance adjustments, and yield predictions are estimates. Published safe minimum temperatures are safety guidance, not optional estimates; ordinary unit conversions use standard math. Appliances and ingredients vary. Check food with a thermometer and follow the applicable safe-temperature and storage guidance. Freezer quality windows assume food was safely handled and kept continuously frozen. The app's Data Sources page links to its government, university extension, measurement, and ingredient-data references. Recipes served with raw or undercooked eggs need pasteurized eggs or pasteurized egg products, as explained in the FDA's egg-safety guidance.
