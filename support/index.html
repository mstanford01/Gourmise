Camera access

Camera access is requested only when you start a scan or turn on Hands-Free Wave during Cook Along. If access was denied, open the iOS Settings app, choose Gourmise, and enable Camera. You can also choose existing photos for cookbook, package, and grocery-list scans.

iCloud Sync

Turn on iCloud Sync in Gourmise Settings, then close and reopen the app. Use the same Apple Account on each device. Sync timing is managed by iCloud and may not be instant.

Back up your library

Open Gourmise Settings, choose Backup & Export, then select Export Full Library Backup. The backup includes your recipes, photos, original scans, ratings, notes, meal plans, grocery lists, favorites, and cooking history.

OCR results need correction

Use a bright, sharp photo with the page or instruction panel filling the frame. Avoid glare and heavy shadows. Gourmise always shows recognized recipe and grocery content for review before saving. Package scanning asks you to choose a cooking method when more than one method is found.

Food safety

Cooking times, temperatures, conversions, storage windows, and other calculated results are estimates. Appliances and ingredients vary. Always check doneness and safe internal temperature with a food thermometer before eating. USDA safe minimum temperatures are available in the Reference tab.
