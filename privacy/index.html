Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

Gourmise is designed to work without a Gourmise account. The app has no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking.

Information Gourmise stores

Recipes you create, recipe photos, cooking photos and captions, saved original scans, notes, ratings, favorites, collections, cooking history, meal plans, grocery lists, timers, drafts, and app preferences are stored on your device. This information is used to provide the features you choose to use.

Camera and photo library

Gourmise can use the camera or photos you select to scan cookbook pages, package instructions, and handwritten grocery lists. Apple Vision performs text recognition on the device. Images and recognized text are not sent to the developer.

If you enable Hands-Free Wave in Settings and then turn it on during Cook Along, the app uses the front camera to detect a hand-wave. Frames are processed on the device and discarded. The camera feed is not recorded, saved, or transmitted.

Saved videos and optional Apple Intelligence

You can select a saved video to read its on-screen recipe text or transcribe its audio. Gourmise uses Apple's on-device Vision and Speech technology. It does not upload your video or audio to a developer server or cloud transcription service. Supported languages and devices vary. With your permission, Apple may download speech-language files needed for local transcription. Temporary working copies are removed when processing finishes or is canceled; text or selected recipe images you choose to save remain in your library.

On supported devices, optional Apple Intelligence features use Apple's Foundation Models framework on your device to help organize imported text or search your saved recipes. Gourmise does not send this content to an external AI service. Results can be wrong and are shown for review. Ordinary scanning, manual entry, and text search remain available without Apple Intelligence.

Optional iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your personal recipes, recipe and cooking photos, saved original scans, grocery lists, meal plans, collections, ratings, notes, and cooking history can sync through your private iCloud database. That data belongs to you, counts against your iCloud storage, and is not visible to the developer through Apple's developer portal. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy apply. Sync requires an available Apple Account, connection, and storage. It is not a substitute for keeping a separate backup file.

Optional household sharing

Household sharing is a separate, optional CloudKit feature. If you create or join a household, recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, and related details you intentionally share are available to the invited participants under the share's permissions. Your private library is not automatically published to everyone. Apple's sharing interface handles invitations and access management. Participants may keep copies of information they have already received. There is no public recipe feed or public profile directory.

Recipe imports and external websites

If you choose Import a Link, Gourmise requests the address you provide and follows supported redirects to read available recipe content. The websites involved may receive connection information such as your IP address and apply their own privacy policies. Gourmise does not route these requests through a developer server, bypass a website's login, or sign you into social networks. Public social posts may not include enough recipe information to import.

The Share extension keeps links and text you send to Gourmise in the app's local Import Inbox until you review or remove them. Opening source, credit, support, or policy links also contacts the selected external website. GitHub Pages hosts Gourmise's public website under GitHub's own privacy practices.

Notifications, Siri, and Shortcuts

Timer and preparation reminders use local notifications. Optional Lock Screen timers and widgets may show recipe or timer names outside the app. You can turn Lock Screen Timers off in Gourmise Settings. If you enable Siri & Shortcuts, Apple's Siri and Shortcuts services process your request under Apple's privacy terms. Gourmise does not receive a recording of your Siri request.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Gourmise does not use your information to track you across apps or websites.

Deleting or exporting your information

You can edit or delete saved content in Gourmise. Settings includes Backup & Export for a portable library file. Backup files can include photos, scans, and personal notes, are not password-protected by Gourmise, and can be read by anyone you give them to. Printing, exporting, or sharing sends the selected content to the destination you choose. Household records use their separate iCloud store and are not included in the personal library backup.

Removing Gourmise removes local app data according to iOS behavior and can remove your only local copy. Export important content first. If you enabled iCloud Sync, synchronized data may remain in iCloud until removed through the app or your iCloud storage controls. Leaving a household or stopping sharing does not erase copies participants already saved. There is no Gourmise account to delete.

Children

Gourmise is not directed to children. The bundled catalog includes recipes for alcoholic drinks, and the App Store age rating reflects that content.

Changes to this policy

If Gourmise’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.
