Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026

Gourmise is designed to work without a Gourmise account. The app has no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking.

Information Gourmise stores

Recipes you create, recipe photos, saved original scans, notes, ratings, favorites, meal plans, grocery lists, timers, and app preferences are stored on your device. This information is used only to provide the features you choose to use.

Camera and photo library

Gourmise can use the camera or photos you select to scan cookbook pages, package instructions, and handwritten grocery lists. Apple Vision performs text recognition on the device. Images and recognized text are not sent to the developer.

If you enable Gesture Prompter and then turn on Hands-Free Wave during Cook Along, the app uses the front camera to detect a hand-wave. Frames are processed on the device and immediately discarded. The camera feed is not recorded, saved, or transmitted.

Optional iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your user recipes, recipe photos, saved original scans, grocery lists, meal plans, ratings, and notes are stored in your private iCloud database so they can sync across your Apple devices. That data belongs to you, counts against your iCloud storage, and is not visible to the developer through Apple’s developer portal. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to this service.

Recipe imports and external websites

If you choose Import from URL, Gourmise sends a direct request from your device to the web address you provide so it can read the recipe. That website may receive normal connection information such as your IP address and may apply its own privacy policy. Gourmise does not route the request through a developer server.

The app also contains links to recipe sources, credits, and other external pages. A website is contacted only after you choose to open it.

Notifications, Siri, and Shortcuts

Timer alerts are local notifications created on your device. If you enable Siri & Shortcuts, Apple’s Siri and Shortcuts services process your request under Apple’s privacy terms. Gourmise does not receive a recording of your voice.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your information and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Gourmise does not use your information to track you across apps or websites.

Deleting or exporting your information

You can edit or delete your saved content in Gourmise. Settings also includes Backup & Export so you can create a portable copy of your library. Removing Gourmise deletes local app data according to iOS behavior. If you enabled iCloud Sync, synchronized data may remain in your iCloud account until it is removed through the app or your iCloud storage controls.

Children

Gourmise is not directed to children. The bundled catalog includes recipes for alcoholic drinks, and the App Store age rating reflects that content.

Changes to this policy

If Gourmise’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will be revised.
